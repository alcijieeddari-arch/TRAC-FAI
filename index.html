About The System

Designed for Trust & Transparency

The Trac-Fai E-Voting System is built to modernize organizational elections with security, fairness, and accessibility at its core.

Bank-Level Security

Your vote is encrypted and anonymized. Even system administrators cannot see who you voted for — only that you voted.

AES-256 Encrypted JWT Auth Vote Verified
Real-Time Results
Live vote tally as ballots are cast

One Person, One Vote

Our system ensures each registered voter can only cast one vote per election, preventing fraud and ensuring fairness.

Vote From Anywhere

Access the voting platform from your phone, tablet, or computer. Fully responsive and mobile-friendly interface.

Instant Results

Votes are counted automatically in real-time. Results are available immediately after polls close.

Full Audit Trail

Complete activity logs and audit trails ensure accountability and allow post-election verification.
